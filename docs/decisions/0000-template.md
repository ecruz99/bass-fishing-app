# ADR NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Issue:** #N

Write an ADR when a decision is hard to reverse, affects several parts of the system, or an interviewer is likely to ask "why?" Keep it to one page (about 400 words). Delete this paragraph.

## Context
What problem or question forced a decision? What constraints matter?

## Decision
What was decided, in a few sentences. Link to the plan for details instead of repeating them.

## Alternatives considered
- **Alternative:** why it was rejected.

## Consequences
What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work this creates.
